wearNothing

wearNothing ek simple idea se shuru hua — kuch apna karne ka, kuch hatke sochne ka. Yeh brand abhi tayar ho raha hai, design ho raha hai, aur apne pehle version ki taraf badh raha hai. Har design, har plan mein ek hi chhupi baat hai: comfort, freedom aur apni soch ko express karna.

Yeh site aur apk Open-source tools or ai-based technologies ke help se banaya gaya hai, jahan hum technology ka use karke apne sapne sach karne ki koshish kar rahe hain.

WorkEasy

"Har kaam asaan ho sakta hai — bas ek sahi help chahiye."

WorkEasy ek aisa idea hai jo humare aas-paas ki chhoti-chhoti zarurat se nikla — jaise room clean karwana ho, cook chahiye ho, ya haircut karwana ho — har kaam ke liye ek local solution. Hum chahte hain ki chhote towns se leke bade shehron tak, har jagah par log ek dusre ki help se apna kaam asaan bana sakein. Yeh app issi vision ke sath develop ho raha hai — simple, easy aur reliable.

Ye bhi Open-source tools or ai-based technologies ke through tayar kiya gaya hai, jisme humne har chhoti detailing par dhyan diya hai taaki user experience behtar ho.

Blog – Humari Soch, Humara Safar

"Shuruaat chhoti thi, par sapne bade hain..."

Humara safar ek simple se room se shuru hua — ek chhoti jagah jahan ideas bante the, bikharte the, fir naye roop me laut kar aate the. Kabhi failures mile, kabhi kisi ne hans diya... lekin kabhi kisi ek ne tarif kar di, to vishwas fir se jeet jaata tha.

Rohit, Shivam, Sanu aur Sheshnath — ye sirf dost nahi, meri team ka wo hissa hain jo har kadam par saath chale. Unhone hamesha mujhe motivate kiya, naye ideas pe feedback diya, aur iss safar ko mere liye mushkil nahi, khaas banaya.

Learning App ko humne IIT Kharagpur ke competition me present kiya. Last round tak pahunche, winner nahi ban paye… lekin har stage ne ek naya lesson diya. Aur aaj hum wearNothing aur WorkEasy jaise ideas par kaam kar rahe hain — jinke peeche sirf coding ya design nahi, balki sapne hain... bade sapne.

Yeh safar abhi bhi likha jaa raha hai — aur aap bhi uska ek hissa ban sakte ho. Ho sakta hai kal yahi kuch lines kisi aur dreamer ka raasta ban jaayein.

To agar aap bhi is soch se judte ho… is sapne ko apna maanna chahte ho… to ek chhoti si shuruaat kariye — WearNothing ya WorkEasy download kariye, aur humein bataiye ki aapko kya feel hua. Shayad yahi ek click humare aur aapke beech ek naya rishta bana de.
